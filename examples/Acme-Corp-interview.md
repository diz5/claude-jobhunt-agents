- 招聘官画像：Acme 初筛由 recruiter 打，30 分钟，非技术，重点确认动机 / 定级 / 薪资 / 时间线。

## 高频问题 + 作答要点 (likely questions → English talking points)

**1. Why are you looking / why leave your current company?**
> "It's been a great run — I've grown a lot and I'm proud of the platform. I'm looking for a bigger
> surface area and more ownership over the payments direction, which is exactly what this role offers."
- 中文要点：正面、向前看；只讲"想要更大 scope"，绝不抱怨现雇主 / 老板 / 组织。

**2. Why Acme / this role?**
> "Two things — the problem and the stage. I've spent years on payment reliability, and Acme is doing
> it as the core product, not a side system. And Series-C is my sweet spot: real scale, still shapeable."
- 中文要点：具体到"支付可靠性 + C 轮阶段"，用公司快照的弹药，不要泛泛"你们很酷"。

**3. Walk me through a recent project.**
> "The transactions-platform migration: we were hitting scaling limits on a monolith. I designed the
> service split, drove the cutover with zero customer-facing downtime, and we came out at 40% lower
> p99 and 3x headroom. I also mentored two engineers through their first big design docs on it."
- 中文要点：impact-first + 量化 + Senior 信号（主导 + 带人）；不要陷进技术细节，招聘官不考技术。

**4. What are you looking for in your next role?**
> "Ownership of a meaningful slice of the platform, a strong eng culture, and room to grow toward
> staff-level impact — driving direction across teams, not just within one."
- 中文要点：把"想要什么"说成 Senior→Staff 成长信号，与该岗对齐。

**5. Anything about the role that concerns you?**
> "Nothing blocking — I'd just want to understand how the team splits ownership across the payments
> services, so I know where I'd have the most impact early." （把顾虑转成聪明的澄清问题）

## 💰 薪资谈话脚本 (comp talk-track)
- **给区间，不给单点**：target **base $190K–210K，total comp $260K–300K**，锚定候选人 benchmark
  （`profile.local.md`）并按 Remote/岗位城市 COL + 州税折算（复用 `job-analyses/Acme-Corp.md` 的估算）。
- **English line**: "Based on my experience and the market for senior backend roles, I'm targeting
  total comp in the **$260K–300K** range, but I'd like to understand the level and scope before we
  anchor on a number." （先给区间 + deferral，不先报低锚。）
- Gating 问题一句话：in-range? → "Yes, that works." · sponsorship? → "No, I'm authorized, no
  sponsorship needed." · W2? → "Yes."
- 中文提醒：不要主动透露现工资具体数字，除非它高于目标且有利；别被"你现在多少"套进低锚。
